## Weekly seminars

Flagstaff has more good science talks than you have free hours. Here's where to find them ([campus map](https://in.nau.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/187/2019-GIS-Reference-Map-ITS150B.pdf)):

**SES Seminars.** 11:30 to 12:30 Wednesdays in the fall; 4 to 5 pm Thursdays in the spring. [Current schedule](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1Rl21TyORV8jWRA2Sy7Zulg_MiCPGMbaEO8x7IFwvY1A/edit?usp=sharing). To join the listserv, email [LISTSERV@LISTS.NAU.EDU](mailto:LISTSERV@LISTS.NAU.EDU) with the message `SUBSCRIBE seses-seminars-l Your_first_name Your_last_name`.

**Forestry Seminars.** 4 to 5 pm Wednesdays year-round, Building 82 Room 017. Email [FGSA@nau.edu](mailto:FGSA@nau.edu) to join the listserv.

**Biology Seminars.** 2:20 to 3:35 pm Mondays year-round, Building 21 Room 256 or 265. Email [biology@nau.edu](mailto:biology@nau.edu) to join the listserv.

**Ecoinformatics Seminars.** 11:30 to 12:30 Mondays, Building 90 Room 102. [Current schedule](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1fvOBcvCOI8b9r1eWFK05PH9-wFNTO1RMeuC2bWpAzu8/edit?usp=sharing). To join the listserv, email [LISTSERV@LISTS.NAU.EDU](mailto:LISTSERV@LISTS.NAU.EDU) with the message `SUBSCRIBE SICCS-WEEKLY-SEMINAR Your_first_name Your_last_name`.
