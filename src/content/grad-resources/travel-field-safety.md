## Advisor-advisee expectations

Every student and advisor should work through the [SES Agreement on Advisor-Advisee Relationship Expectations](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1bPz4W-42rteaoZmxrOFHgDVz6KRO1VMX) together.

## Before you travel: do these first

**Become an Authorized Driver.** Required to drive NAU-rented vehicles or be reimbursed for personal vehicle mileage. Start at [Environmental Health and Safety](https://in.nau.edu/environmental-health-and-safety/occupational-safety/).

**Annual Travel Certification (ATC).** Mandatory for all NAU employees and students who travel, renewed every fiscal year (July 1 to June 30). Complete it in the [ATC portal](https://nau.service-now.com/hr?id=sc_cat_item&sys_id=a041b94e1bcf9250524b635b234bcbc3&sysparm_category=91e5c690db6f44105e69d2e3ca9619e3).

**Travel Ticket.** An approved Travel Ticket is required **before** making any travel arrangements. Coordinate with your advisor (or Janelle) on funding, then submit via the [Travel Ticket portal](https://nau.service-now.com/hr?id=sc_cat_item&sys_id=5ba506dcdb2f44105e69d2e3ca96199a&referrer=popular_items).

**Vehicle rentals.** Consult your advisor about the payment method, then book through [Fleet Services](https://in.nau.edu/university-transit-services/fleet-services/vehicle-rental/).
