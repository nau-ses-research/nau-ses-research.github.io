Given the scale and challenges posed by climate change, there is a strong and growing need for professionals with robust skills in tracking, reporting, and managing carbon emissions from companies and organizations. NAU provides one-of-a-kind, hands-on training in carbon accounting, with pathways for both current students and working professionals.

Both pathways cover the same four online courses, taken in sequence: Foundations in GHG Accounting I, Foundations in GHG Accounting II, Value-Chain GHG Accounting, and GHG Mitigation and Management.

## Choose your pathway

### Graduate Certificate in Greenhouse Gas Accounting (for credit)

- **Credits:** 12 graduate credit hours (EES 591, 592, 593 and 594)
- **Format:** Asynchronous online; four 8-week courses over two semesters
- **Start:** Every Fall semester; rolling admissions
- **Admission:** Bachelor's degree from an accredited institution, minimum 3.0 GPA

**[Graduate certificate details and how to apply](/student-opportunities/graduate-certificates/)**

### GHG Accounting Professional Certificate (non-credit)

- **Format:** Fully online and self-paced; start at any time
- **Cost:** $499 per course, $1,996 for all four
- **For:** Working professionals building skills for a current position or a career change

**[Professional certificate details and registration](/student-opportunities/professional-certificates/)**
