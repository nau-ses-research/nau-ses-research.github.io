## Culturally informed restoration of Madrean oak woodlands

**Funding:** Emory Oak Collaborative Tribal Restoration Initiative (EOCTRI)
Fellowship
**Duration:** 2-year project (MS) or 4-year project (PhD)
**Start date:** Fall 2027

### Project overview

Changing environmental conditions threaten ecological function and cultural
practices linked to Southwestern landscapes. To address this, the Lab of
Conservation Ecology at NAU is recruiting graduate students (MS or PhD) to join
our work on Emory oak (*Quercus emoryi*), a foundational species and culturally
significant tree of Madrean oak woodlands.

The student will work with the Emory Oak Collaborative Tribal Restoration
Initiative (EOCTRI), a long-term partnership between Western Apache Nations,
land managers, and NAU, aimed at conserving and restoring Madrean oak habitat to
preserve the tradition of Apache acorn harvest in the Southwest, and will
contribute to the NSF DISES project (#2206810) focused on linking oak population
dynamics to Indigenous land stewardship, drought, and fire.

### Your role

You will help lead a new phase of data collection and analysis aimed at:

- Managing the demographic census of Emory oak across Arizona
- Supporting the coordination of EOCTRI treatment monitoring
- Assessing the effectiveness of restoration practices aimed at bolstering
  ecological function across heterogeneous landscapes

### The ideal candidate

- Interest in applied ecology aimed at generating data-driven management guidance
- Demonstrated experience in fieldwork
- Commitment to working with diverse populations and community partners
- Desire to learn data analysis and interpretation
- Self-reliance and independence
- Excellent writing skills

### How to apply

Formal applications to the School of Earth and Sustainability (MS in
Environmental Science & Policy; PhD in Earth Sciences and Environmental
Sustainability) are due January 1. To apply to this opportunity, email
Dr. Souther (sara.souther@nau.edu) by November 1 with your CV, a writing sample,
and a brief statement of why the project appeals to you. She will interview
strong, qualified applicants and encourage the top candidates to submit a formal
program application.
