## Amino acid biogeochemistry (1-2 students, NSF funded)

We seek one to two graduate students for NSF-funded research in amino acid
biogeochemistry, focusing on two complementary projects.

**Amino acid geochronology.** Advance racemization dating using marine mollusk
shells to establish temperature-dependent age benchmarks for Last Interglacial
high sea-level deposits.

**Lake sediment biogeochemistry.** Investigate racemization and decomposition
patterns in lake sediment cores to understand organic matter cycling and its
long-term fate.

Students will gain hands-on expertise in biogeochemical analysis, paleoclimate
applications, and data interpretation, with access to state-of-the-art
facilities via the ACE Radiocarbon Lab and opportunities to collaborate with an
interdisciplinary research team.
