## About

Dr. Joseph Byrnes was an Assistant Research Professor in the School of Earth
and Sustainability at Northern Arizona University from 2023 to 2026. A
seismologist, he used recordings of seismic waves to image the Earth's
interior, with a focus on the structure and dynamics of the
lithosphere-asthenosphere system.

## Research

His work at NAU spanned attenuation tomography of the conterminous United
States and the Tonga-Lau subduction system, seismic anisotropy of the Pacific
upper mantle, the architecture of the Wyoming craton, volatiles and melting
along the East African Rift, and transdimensional Bayesian methods for
imaging the crust and upper mantle. He also contributed to the Cascadia
Region Earthquake Science Center (CRESCENT) and to earthquake hazard studies
in the western United States.
