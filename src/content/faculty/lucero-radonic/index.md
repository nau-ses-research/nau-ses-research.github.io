## About

Dr. Lucero Radonic is an Associate Professor at Northern Arizona University,
where she has been on the faculty since 2023. She is an applied
environmental anthropologist working at the intersection of water
governance, infrastructure, and environmental change in Latin America and
the United States.

## Research

Dr. Radonic studies how people gain, lose, and negotiate access to water as
cities grow and the climate changes. Her ethnographic work began with
dissertation research on water management among the Yaquis of Hermosillo,
Sonora, and has grown into a program spanning urban water security, green
infrastructure, and the politics of adaptation. Recent work examines whose
knowledge and values count in green-infrastructure planning, including
bilingual community engagement as a practice of environmental justice, and
uses participatory methods that make community members partners in the
research rather than subjects of it.
