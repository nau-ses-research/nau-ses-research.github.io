## About

Dr. Rosemary Logan teaches in the First Year Seminar Program and the
Sustainable Communities Program at Northern Arizona University, where she
has been a faculty member since 2013. Her work centers on education as a
practice of sustainability: how learning experiences transform the way
people relate to their communities, food systems, and landscapes.

## Teaching and Scholarship

Dr. Logan's scholarship focuses on transformative and experiential learning:
what happens when education moves out of the lecture hall and into gardens,
communities, and long-term mentored projects. Her doctoral research followed
graduates of Expeditionary Learning schools to understand how immersive
schooling shapes later civic and environmental engagement, and her teaching
puts those findings into practice through community-based projects,
permaculture and food-systems work, and first-year seminars that anchor new
students in the ecology and communities of the Colorado Plateau.
